# School Reporting Checklist

## Purpose

This checklist helps HBCU athletes prepare information before reporting NIL activity to their school, athletic department, or compliance office.

School reporting rules vary.

This document does not replace school policy.

It helps athletes organize the information they may need before accepting, signing, posting, or receiving payment from a NIL opportunity.

## Core Principle

Report before there is a problem.

If the school requires NIL disclosure, the athlete should understand what to report, when to report it, and where to submit it.

## Why Reporting Matters

School reporting may help protect:

- Eligibility
- Scholarship status
- Athlete reputation
- School relationships
- Sponsor relationships
- Tax records
- Contract records
- Future NIL opportunities

Reporting also helps athletes build professional habits.

## Reporting Red Flags

Stop and ask compliance if:

- Sponsor wants secrecy.
- Sponsor refuses written terms.
- Payment is tied to performance.
- Payment is tied to enrollment.
- Payment is tied to recruiting.
- Sponsor wants school marks used without approval.
- Sponsor wants lifetime image rights.
- Sponsor wants broad paid ad usage.
- Sponsor asks for account access.
- Sponsor makes token profit promises.
- Athlete does not understand the agreement.
- Reporting deadline is unclear.
- Product or service value is not recorded.
