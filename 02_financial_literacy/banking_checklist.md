# Banking Checklist

## Purpose

This checklist helps HBCU athletes understand basic banking before receiving NIL income, sponsor payments, camp income, appearance fees, or digital payments.

Banking is part of financial literacy.

If athletes are going to earn money, they need a safe and organized way to receive, hold, track, and separate that money.

## Core Principle

Do not treat every payment app like a bank.

Do not treat every deposit like spending money.

Do not mix NIL income with confusion.

A prepared athlete knows where money goes, how it is tracked, and how it is protected.

## Basic Banking Terms

### Checking Account

A checking account is usually used for everyday spending, bills, debit card purchases, and deposits.

### Savings Account

A savings account is usually used to hold money for goals, emergencies, taxes, or future needs.

### Debit Card

A debit card spends money from a checking account.

It is not free money.

### Direct Deposit

Direct deposit sends money electronically into an account.

### Bank Statement

A bank statement shows deposits, withdrawals, fees, and account activity.

### Overdraft

An overdraft happens when someone spends more money than is available.

Overdrafts may create fees.

### Account Fee

Some accounts charge monthly fees, overdraft fees, ATM fees, transfer fees, or minimum balance fees.

### Routing Number

A routing number identifies the bank.

### Account Number

An account number identifies the specific account.

Athletes should protect account numbers and share them only when appropriate and secure.

## Before Opening an Account

Ask:

- Is this bank or credit union trusted?
- Are there monthly fees?
- Are there overdraft fees?
- Is there a minimum balance?
- Is there a student account option?
- Are there ATM fees?
- Is online banking available?
- Is mobile deposit available?
- Is customer support available?
- Can I open both checking and savings?
- Can I separate NIL money from personal spending?

A simple structure may include:

### Checking Account

Used for:

- Food
- Transportation
- Bills
- Personal spending
- Normal deposits

### Savings Account

Used for:

- Emergency fund
- Future goals
- Money not meant for daily spending

### Tax Set-Aside Category

Some athletes may use a separate savings account or budgeting category for possible taxes.

Used for:

- NIL income tax planning
- Tax preparation
- Future obligations

This is educational only.

Ask a qualified professional for tax-specific guidance.

## NIL Payment Deposit Checklist

When NIL money arrives:

- Record the payment.
- Save proof of payment.
- Confirm sponsor name.
- Confirm amount.
- Confirm date.
- Confirm payment method.
- Move tax set-aside money if needed.
- Move savings if planned.
- Update NIL deal tracker.
- Save agreement and screenshots.
- Check whether school reporting is complete.

## Payment Methods

Athletes may receive payment through:

- Check
- Direct deposit
- Digital payment app
- Cash
- Gift card
- Product
- Service
- Affiliate payout
- Marketplace platform

Each method should be tracked.

## Cash Payment Safety

If paid in cash:

- Count it.
- Record it.
- Ask for a receipt or written confirmation.
- Deposit it if appropriate.
- Save notes about the deal.
- Do not treat cash as invisible.
- Track it like any other income.

Cash still matters.

## Digital Payment App Checklist

For payment apps, track:

- App name
- Sponsor name
- Username or sender
- Amount received
- Date
- Purpose
- Screenshot
- Transfer to bank, if applicable
- Fees, if any

Avoid leaving large amounts sitting in payment apps without understanding protections and transfer rules.

## Protecting Account Information

Do not share:

- Online banking password
- Debit card PIN
- Full account number casually
- Verification codes
- One-time login codes
- Security answers
- Private banking screenshots

Be careful with anyone who asks for account access.

Sponsors do not need your banking password.

## Fraud Warning Signs

Be careful if someone:

- Sends a check for too much money
- Asks you to send some money back
- Wants your banking password
- Wants your verification code
- Offers payment before explaining the deal
- Pressures you to act quickly
- Uses confusing payment instructions
- Refuses to identify themselves
- Says the deal must stay secret
- Wants you to use your account for someone else’s money

If it feels strange, stop and ask for help.

## Debit Card Discipline

A debit card makes spending easy.

Before swiping, ask:

- Is this a need or want?
- Did I track my income?
- Did I set aside money for taxes?
- Did I save first?
- Do I know my balance?
- Will this cause a fee?
- Will I regret this purchase tomorrow?

## Bank Statement Review

Review statements each month.

Look for:

- Deposits
- Sponsor payments
- Fees
- Unexpected charges
- Duplicate charges
- Subscriptions
- ATM fees
- Transfers
- Spending patterns
- Possible fraud

Monthly review helps athletes understand where money is going.

## Banking Questions for Athletes

Ask:

- Where does my NIL money go?
- Do I have savings separate from spending?
- Do I know my account fees?
- Do I check statements?
- Do I track deposits?
- Do I protect passwords?
- Do I know how to spot fraud?
- Do I save tax money?
- Do I understand digital payment apps?
- Do I have a plan before spending?

## Banking Red Flags

Slow down if:

- You do not know your account balance.
- You overdraft often.
- You pay frequent fees.
- You leave money untracked in payment apps.
- You share passwords.
- You ignore statements.
- You spend deposits immediately.
- You cannot separate savings from spending.
- You use digital payments with no records.
- You receive strange payment instructions.
