# Budgeting Template

## Purpose

This template helps HBCU athletes create a simple budget for NIL income, school expenses, personal needs, and long-term goals.

A budget is not punishment.

A budget is a plan.

It tells money where to go before it disappears.

## Core Principle

Every dollar needs a job.

That job may be:

- Bills
- Food
- Transportation
- Savings
- Taxes
- Emergency fund
- Debt payment
- Brand reinvestment
- Giving
- Personal spending

The goal is not to spend nothing.

The goal is to spend with awareness.

Budget Red Flags

Slow down if:

* You do not know how much money you have.
* You spend NIL money before tracking it.
* You forget to save for taxes.
* You do not know when bills are due.
* You borrow money for wants.
* You use credit without understanding interest.
* You accept a deal but spend more than the deal pays.
* You do not save agreements or receipts.
* You do not know where your money went.
