# Content Calendar Template

## Purpose

This template helps HBCU athletes plan social media content for NIL, personal branding, sponsor campaigns, community storytelling, and AND1 DAO participation.

Social media should not be random.

It should be planned enough to protect the athlete’s reputation while still feeling real.

The goal is not to make athletes fake.

The goal is to help athletes show their real story with discipline.

## Core Principle

Content is infrastructure.

A strong content calendar helps athletes:

- Stay consistent
- Tell their story
- Support sponsors
- Show leadership
- Highlight HBCU culture
- Build audience trust
- Avoid rushed posts
- Track deliverables
- Protect reputation
- Prepare for NIL opportunities

## Caption Formula

Use this simple formula:

1. Hook
2. Story or message
3. Sponsor or community connection
4. Call to action
5. Disclosure, if required

Example:

Game week starts with discipline.

Stopped by [Business Name] before Saturday because they support HBCU athletes and local community.

If you are near campus, show them love.

Paid NIL partnership with [Business Name].

## Disclosure Reminder

Sponsored content may require clear disclosure.

Possible language:

- Paid partnership with [Business Name]
- Sponsored by [Business Name]
- NIL partnership with [Business Name]
- Advertisement / ad, where required
- Thank you to [Business Name] for sponsoring this NIL partnership

Athletes should follow school, platform, and applicable disclosure rules.

## Content Safety Checklist

Before posting, ask:

- Does this fit my values?
- Is the sponsor tagged correctly?
- Is disclosure needed?
- Did I follow school rules?
- Am I using school logos, uniforms, facilities, or marks?
- Do I have permission to use this music, photo, or video?
- Is anyone else in the content?
- Do I need their permission?
- Could this hurt my reputation?
- Did I save proof for the sponsor?
- Did I track the deal?

## AND1 DAO Connection

Content calendars support AND1 DAO utility because social media marketing is one of the DAO’s core pillars.

DAO contributors can help athletes by:

- Creating calendars
- Editing captions
- Building campaign plans
- Tracking deliverables
- Creating short-form prompts
- Supporting sponsor posts
- Documenting community activations
- Turning athlete stories into campaigns
- Creating financial literacy content
- Building weekly social media themes

Eat what you kill means contributors create value through useful execution.

A content calendar turns effort into structure.
