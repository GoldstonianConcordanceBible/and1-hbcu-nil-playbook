# Code of Conduct

## Purpose

The AND1 HBCU NIL Playbook exists to support underserved athletes, especially HBCU student-athletes, through education, financial literacy, participation, and social media marketing infrastructure.

This community must remain respectful, safe, transparent, and athlete-centered.

## Expected Behavior

Participants are expected to:

- Respect HBCU athletes, alumni, coaches, schools, and communities
- Communicate professionally
- Prioritize athlete protection
- Avoid misleading claims
- Support education and financial literacy
- Credit contributors
- Be honest about limitations
- Respect compliance requirements
- Protect student-athlete eligibility
- Encourage transparency
- Build with integrity
- Help athletes understand opportunity before chasing money
- Treat culture as something to protect, not exploit

## Unacceptable Behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment
- Exploitation
- Discrimination
- Threats
- Doxxing
- Predatory dealmaking
- Misleading financial claims
- Guaranteed token return claims
- Guaranteed NIL income claims
- Pressure to sign agreements quickly
- Encouraging athletes to hide deals
- Using athletes without consent
- Misusing athlete images
- Disrespecting HBCU institutions or communities
- Treating student-athletes as content products instead of people
- Creating sponsor models that harm athlete eligibility or reputation

## Athlete Protection Standard

Athletes should never be pressured into:

- Signing unclear agreements
- Giving away lifetime image rights
- Accepting unfair compensation
- Hiding deals from compliance offices
- Promoting products they do not understand
- Participating in campaigns that harm their reputation
- Taking financial or legal risks without advice
- Sharing private information with unverified partners
- Accepting payment without tracking income
- Posting sponsor content without understanding disclosure requirements

## Sponsor Conduct Standard

Sponsors should:

- Respect athlete schedules
- Pay fairly
- Define deliverables clearly
- Avoid vague agreements
- Avoid pressure tactics
- Respect school compliance rules
- Avoid using athlete images without permission
- Provide written terms
- Honor payment timelines
- Support athlete education

Sponsors should not attempt to use NIL as a way to control, manipulate, or exploit student-athletes.

## DAO Participation Standard

The AND1 DAO follows an **eat what you kill** participation model.

This means contributors earn reputation through work, not status.

DAO participants should not claim:

- Guaranteed rewards
- Guaranteed income
- Guaranteed NIL deals
- Guaranteed token appreciation
- Guaranteed sponsor access
- Guaranteed eligibility outcomes
- Guaranteed financial returns
- Guaranteed passive income

Participation should be based on education, execution, documentation, contribution, and community service.

## Social Media Conduct Standard

Social media is a core part of this project.

Participants should use social media to:

- Educate
- Promote athletes responsibly
- Highlight HBCU culture
- Support financial literacy
- Thank sponsors
- Document community impact
- Share accurate information
- Build trust

Participants should not use social media to:

- Harass athletes
- Shame athletes
- Spread false claims
- Misrepresent NIL deals
- Promote guaranteed income
- Misuse athlete images
- Pressure athletes publicly
- Create misleading token hype
- Damage school or athlete reputation
