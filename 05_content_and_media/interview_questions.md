# Interview Questions

## Purpose

This document gives HBCU athletes, mentors, creators, sponsors, alumni, and AND1 DAO contributors a question bank for interviews, podcasts, social media clips, athlete spotlights, speaker sessions, and NIL storytelling.

Good questions help athletes tell better stories.

Better stories help athletes build stronger brands, stronger sponsor relationships, and stronger community connection.

## Core Principle

The best interview does not exploit the athlete.

It helps the athlete explain who they are, what they represent, and what they are building.

## How to Use This Question Bank

Use these questions for:

- Athlete spotlight videos
- Podcast interviews
- Media kit bios
- Sponsor content
- Alumni mentor sessions
- Financial literacy campaigns
- HBCU culture stories
- NIL education videos
- Community activation recaps
- AND1 DAO contributor content

Do not ask every question at once.

Choose the questions that fit the purpose.

## Interview Prep for Athletes

Before an interview, athletes should know:

- Who is interviewing them
- Where the interview will appear
- Whether it is recorded
- Whether clips will be used later
- Whether sponsors are involved
- Whether compensation is involved
- Whether school approval is needed
- Whether team rules apply
- What topics are off-limits
- What message they want to communicate

## Interview Safety Checklist

Before publishing an interview, confirm:

- Athlete approved the interview.
- Athlete knows where it will be posted.
- Sponsor involvement is clear.
- Disclosure language is included if needed.
- No private team information is shared.
- No school marks are used without permission.
- No private family details are shared without permission.
- No misleading financial claims are made.
- No token profit promises are made.
- No legal, tax, or financial advice is presented outside qualifications.
- Athlete is comfortable with the final message.

## Consent Reminder

Athletes should understand how their interview may be used.

Clarify:

- Will the full interview be posted?
- Will short clips be created?
- Can the sponsor repost it?
- Can it be used in ads?
- How long can it be used?
- Can the athlete request edits?
- Will the athlete be tagged?
- Is compensation involved?
