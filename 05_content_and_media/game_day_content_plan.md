# Game-Day Content Plan

## Purpose

This template helps HBCU athletes plan responsible game-day content for personal branding, NIL partnerships, sponsor activations, community storytelling, and HBCU culture documentation.

Game day creates attention.

Attention can create opportunity.

But game-day content must be planned carefully because athletes must protect:

- Eligibility
- Team rules
- School marks
- Sponsor obligations
- Reputation
- Focus
- Time
- Privacy
- Compliance responsibilities

## Core Principle

Game day is not just content.

Game day is performance, preparation, team responsibility, and school representation.

Content should support the athlete’s brand without distracting from the game.

## Content Safety Reminder

Before game-day posting, confirm:

- I understand team rules.
- I understand school NIL rules.
- I know whether I can use uniforms.
- I know whether I can use facilities.
- I know whether I can use school logos.
- I know whether I can use game footage.
- I know whether sponsor content is allowed.
- I know whether disclosure is required.
- I know whether the post must be reported.
- I will not post private team information.
- I will not post anything that distracts from preparation.

## During-Game Content

Athletes should be careful during games.

The athlete’s priority is the team.

Do not post if it violates team rules, distracts from preparation, or creates compliance problems.

Possible during-game content should usually come from:

- Approved team media
- Approved school content
- Approved fan content
- Pre-scheduled sponsor post
- Non-distracting story repost, if allowed

## During-Game Safety Questions

Before posting during the event, ask:

- Am I allowed to post right now?
- Does this distract from the team?
- Does this show private team information?
- Does this use school content without permission?
- Is this sponsor content?
- Does it need disclosure?
- Could this be misunderstood by coaches or compliance?

If unsure, wait.

## Game-Day Content Timeline

### 48 Hours Before Game

- Confirm sponsor deliverables.
- Confirm school NIL reporting.
- Draft captions.
- Prepare photos or videos.
- Confirm disclosure language.
- Review team rules.
- Schedule content if appropriate.

### 24 Hours Before Game

- Finalize posts.
- Save sponsor tags.
- Confirm payment terms.
- Confirm content deadlines.
- Avoid rushed posting.
- Prioritize rest and preparation.

### Game Day Morning

- Post only planned content.
- Keep content simple.
- Do not let posting distract from routine.
- Save proof of sponsor posts.

### After Game

- Post reflection or sponsor thank-you, if planned.
- Avoid emotional posting.
- Avoid blaming teammates, coaches, officials, or opponents.
- Save analytics and screenshots.
- Update deal tracker.

### Next Day

- Review content performance.
- Send proof to sponsor.
- Confirm payment.
- Track income.
- Save records.
- Reflect on what worked.

## Game-Day Do Not Post List

Avoid posting:

- Private team meetings
- Locker room content without approval
- Injury details
- Coach strategy
- Playbooks
- Travel information that creates safety risk
- Negative comments about teammates
- Negative comments about coaches
- Negative comments about officials
- Trash talk that damages reputation
- Sponsor content without disclosure, where required
- School marks used without approval
- Music or footage you do not have rights to use

The real win is learning how to manage what you earn.
