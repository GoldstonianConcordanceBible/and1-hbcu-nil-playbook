# Short-Form Video Prompts

## Purpose

This document gives HBCU athletes, creators, sponsors, mentors, and AND1 DAO contributors short-form video ideas for NIL campaigns, personal branding, financial literacy, community storytelling, and social media growth.

Short-form video is the modern mixtape.

AND1 used VHS to show the world streetball culture.

Today, athletes can use TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, and other platforms to show their story directly.

## Core Principle

Every short video should have a purpose.

That purpose may be to:

- Tell a story
- Teach a lesson
- Thank a sponsor
- Show training
- Promote a local business
- Highlight HBCU culture
- Build audience trust
- Share financial literacy
- Support community activation
- Document athlete growth

Do not post only to post.

Post with purpose.

## Short-Form Video Formula

A simple video can follow this structure:

1. Hook
2. Visual proof
3. Short story or message
4. Sponsor or community connection, if applicable
5. Call to action
6. Disclosure, if required

Example:

Hook:

Game week starts before the lights come on.

Visual proof:

Training clip, meal prep, shoes being tied, gym entrance.

Message:

Discipline is what nobody sees before everybody watches.

Sponsor connection:

Thank you to [Business Name] for supporting HBCU athletes.

Disclosure:

Paid NIL partnership with [Business Name].

## Video Length

Suggested lengths:

- 7 to 15 seconds for quick moments
- 15 to 30 seconds for sponsor content
- 30 to 60 seconds for storytelling
- 60 to 90 seconds for education or interviews

Short does not mean empty.

Short means focused.

## Athlete Story Prompts

### Prompt 1: Why I Chose My HBCU

Hook:

Content:

Possible angles:

- Coach relationship
- Culture
- Opportunity

## Financial Literacy Prompts

### Prompt 11: Track Before Spending

Hook:

Before you spend NIL money, track it.

Message:

Write down who paid you, how much, when, and what you did for it.

### Prompt 12: Save Before Spending

Hook:

The first move after getting paid should not be shopping.

Message:

Set aside taxes. Save something. Cover needs. Then decide what to spend.

### Prompt 13: NIL Taxes Awareness

Hook:

NIL money may come with tax responsibilities.

Message:

Do not assume the full payment is spending money. Save records and ask a qualified professional if needed.

### Prompt 14: Emergency Fund Challenge

Hook:

Message:

An emergency fund helps protect you when life changes fast.

### Prompt 15: Needs vs Wants

Hook:

Is it a need or a want?

Message:

Needs keep you stable. Wants are fine, but they should not destroy the plan.

### Prompt 16: Quiet Money Win

Hook:

Not every money win gets posted.

Examples:

- Paid a bill
- Saved $50
- Tracked income
- Avoided impulse spending
- Set aside tax money
- Read before signing

## Content Safety Checklist

Before posting, ask:

- Does this fit my values?
- Does this protect my reputation?
- Is this sponsor content?
- Do I need disclosure?
- Did I follow school NIL rules?
- Am I using school marks, uniforms, facilities, or logos?
- Do I have permission to use all music, photos, and videos?
- Is anyone else in the video?
- Do I need permission from them?
- Did I save proof?
- Did I track payment?
- Would I be proud of this post later?
