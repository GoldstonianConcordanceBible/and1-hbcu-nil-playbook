# Alumni Deal Network

## Purpose

This document explains how alumni can help build responsible NIL opportunities for HBCU athletes.

The alumni deal network is not only about money.

It is about turning alumni pride into athlete infrastructure.

HBCU alumni can become the bridge between student-athletes and ethical community commerce.

## Core Principle

Alumni are infrastructure.

They know the school.

They know the culture.

They know the community.

They know the businesses.

They know the road.

They can help athletes access opportunities that would otherwise remain hidden.

## Why Alumni Matter

Alumni can support NIL because they often have:

- School loyalty
- Community relationships
- Business ownership
- Professional experience
- Local influence
- Mentorship ability
- Financial knowledge
- Media networks
- Event access
- Donor relationships
- Family trust
- Long-term commitment to the institution

Many HBCU athletes do not need strangers first.

They need structured access to the people already connected to the school.

## Alumni Deal Network Mission

The mission is to connect HBCU athletes with responsible local, regional, and alumni-supported NIL opportunities while protecting athletes through education, financial literacy, and compliance awareness.

## What the Network Does

The alumni deal network helps:

- Identify alumni-owned businesses
- Identify local sponsor opportunities
- Educate sponsors about NIL
- Prepare athletes before outreach
- Review sponsor fit
- Create community campaigns
- Host networking events
- Support financial literacy workshops
- Build athlete media kits
- Connect mentors and athletes
- Track impact
- Document success stories

## What the Network Does Not Do

The alumni deal network does not:

- Guarantee NIL deals
- Guarantee payment
- Guarantee sponsor interest
- Guarantee eligibility
- Guarantee token rewards
- Replace school compliance
- Provide legal advice unless qualified
- Provide tax advice unless qualified
- Pressure athletes into deals
- Pressure businesses into deals
- Use athletes without consent

## Alumni Sponsor Education

Before sponsoring athletes, alumni should understand:

- NIL rules vary by school and state.
- Athletes may need to report deals.
- Written terms protect both sides.
- Payment should be clear.
- Deliverables should be specific.
- Athlete image rights should be limited and fair.
- School marks may require approval.
- Sponsored content may need disclosure.
- Athletes have demanding schedules.
- Financial literacy should be part of the mission.

## Compliance Reminders

Every alumni-supported opportunity should remind athletes:

- Review school NIL policy.
- Report the deal if required.
- Ask compliance before using school marks.
- Understand social media disclosure rules.
- Save written terms.
- Track payment.
- Save receipts and screenshots.
- Ask qualified professionals when needed.

## Risk Standards

The alumni deal network must avoid:

- Pay-for-play arrangements
- Hidden compensation
- Pressure on athletes
- Use of school marks without permission
- Conflicts of interest without disclosure
- Misleading token claims
- Guaranteed income claims
- Exploitative contracts
- Lifetime image rights
- Unclear payment terms
