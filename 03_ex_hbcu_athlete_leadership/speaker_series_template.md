# Speaker Series Template

## Purpose

This template helps HBCU athletic programs, alumni networks, NIL educators, and AND1 DAO contributors organize speaker sessions for student-athletes.

The speaker series is designed to teach athletes about:

- NIL readiness
- Financial literacy
- Personal branding
- Social media strategy
- Sponsor communication
- Contract red flags
- Career planning
- Leadership
- Entrepreneurship
- Life after sports

The goal is not to create one motivational event.

The goal is to build repeatable education.

## Core Principle

A speaker series should give athletes tools they can use immediately.

Every session should help athletes become more prepared, more protected, and more professional.

## Suggested Series Name

Blacktop to Balance Sheet Speaker Series

## Series Mission

The Blacktop to Balance Sheet Speaker Series helps HBCU athletes turn culture, influence, and opportunity into responsible NIL participation, financial literacy, and long-term personal development.

## Audience

This speaker series is designed for:

- HBCU student-athletes

## Speaker Selection Criteria

A speaker should:

- Respect HBCU culture
- Understand athlete development
- Provide practical value
- Avoid misleading claims
- Avoid guaranteed income promises
- Avoid token hype
- Encourage compliance
- Support financial literacy
- Protect athletes
- Teach from experience or expertise
- Leave athletes with clear next steps

## Session Format

Recommended session length:

60 minutes

Suggested structure:

- 5 minutes: Welcome and purpose
- 10 minutes: Speaker story
- 20 minutes: Main teaching
- 15 minutes: Athlete questions
- 5 minutes: Action step
- 5 minutes: Closing and resources

For longer workshops, use 90 minutes.

## Event Checklist

Before the event:

- Confirm speaker.
- Confirm topic.
- Confirm date and time.
- Confirm location or virtual link.
- Prepare athlete worksheets.
- Confirm recording permissions.
- Confirm photo permissions.
- Share calendar invite.
- Promote to athletes.
- Confirm compliance-sensitive topics.
- Prepare Q&A questions.
- Prepare follow-up resources.

During the event:

- Welcome athletes.
- Introduce speaker.
- State purpose.
- Remind participants this is educational.
- Keep time.
- Capture key lessons.
- Collect questions.
- Share action step.

After the event:

- Send thank-you message.
- Share resources.
- Collect evaluations.
- Save recording if allowed.
- Document attendance.
- Record key lessons.
- Add improvements to repo.
- Invite speaker for future involvement, if appropriate.
