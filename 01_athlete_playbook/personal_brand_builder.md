# Personal Brand Builder

## Purpose

This worksheet helps HBCU athletes define their personal brand before pursuing NIL opportunities.

A strong personal brand makes it easier to know:

- Who you are
- What you represent
- What sponsors fit you
- What content you should create
- What opportunities you should accept
- What opportunities you should reject

NIL begins with identity.

## What Is a Personal Brand?

A personal brand is the public meaning attached to your name.

It includes:

- Your story
- Your values
- Your voice
- Your sport
- Your school
- Your community
- Your personality
- Your work ethic
- Your reputation
- Your online presence
- Your offline behavior

Your brand is not only what you post.

Your brand is what people believe about you.

30-Day Brand Builder Challenge

Week 1: Identity

* Write your brand statement
* Update your bio
* Choose content pillars
* Clean up your public profiles
* Pick five sponsor categories

Week 2: Content

* Post one training clip
* Post one school pride post
* Post one story about your journey
* Post one community or teammate post
* Post one value-based post

Week 3: Sponsor Readiness

* Create a one-page athlete profile
* Build a sponsor list
* Draft an outreach message
* Practice your elevator pitch
* Ask compliance how NIL reporting works

Week 4: Financial Literacy

* Create a basic budget
* Set a savings goal
* Learn tax basics
* Create an income tracker
* Decide what you will do with your first NIL payment

Final Word

Before you chase the deal, define the name.

Before you post for a sponsor, know your values.

Protect it.
