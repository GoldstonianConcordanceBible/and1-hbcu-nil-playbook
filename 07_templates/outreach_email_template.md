# Outreach Email Template

## Purpose

This template helps HBCU athletes, alumni mentors, sponsor development teams, and AND1 DAO contributors contact potential NIL sponsors professionally.

Outreach should be respectful, clear, specific, and easy to answer.

The goal is not to beg for support.

The goal is to create a responsible business conversation.

## Core Principle

A good outreach message shows:

- Who the athlete is
- Why the sponsor fits
- What partnership could look like
- Why the opportunity matters
- How to continue the conversation

## Before Sending Outreach

Complete these steps before contacting a sponsor:

- Review your personal brand.
- Complete your media kit.
- Check sponsor fit.
- Review your school NIL reporting process.
- Prepare possible deliverables.
- Know your basic payment expectations.
- Prepare a deal tracker.
- Be ready to communicate professionally.

## Outreach Rules

Do:

- Be professional
- Be specific
- Personalize the message
- Explain why the sponsor fits
- Offer a simple next step
- Respect no response
- Track every outreach attempt
- Save messages
- Follow school NIL rules

Do not:

- Spam businesses
- Use pressure tactics
- Promise sales
- Promise follower growth
- Promise token returns
- Misrepresent your audience
- Send unprofessional messages
- Hide sponsor communication from compliance if reporting is required
