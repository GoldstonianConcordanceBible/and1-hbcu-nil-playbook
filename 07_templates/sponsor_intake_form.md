# Sponsor Intake Form

## Purpose

This form helps local businesses, alumni-owned companies, community partners, sponsors, and organizations prepare for responsible NIL partnerships with HBCU athletes.

The form collects information about the sponsor, business goals, campaign ideas, budget, deliverables, compliance concerns, usage rights, and financial literacy connection.

The goal is to help sponsors create clear, fair, and athlete-centered opportunities.

## Core Principle

A sponsor should be prepared before contacting an athlete.

Preparation protects both the business and the athlete.

## Sponsor Conduct Agreement

A responsible sponsor agrees to:

- Respect the athlete
- Pay on time
- Put terms in writing
- Define deliverables clearly
- Avoid pressure tactics
- Encourage compliance review
- Avoid hidden deals
- Avoid misleading claims
- Avoid broad image rights without fair compensation
- Avoid asking for account passwords
- Respect athlete schedules
- Support financial literacy where possible

## Sponsor Readiness Checklist

Before launching a campaign, confirm:

- Business goal is clear.
- Athlete fit is clear.
- Deliverables are specific.
- Payment is defined.
- Payment date is defined.
- Usage rights are limited and clear.
- Exclusivity is clear, if included.
- School marks are not used without approval.
- Disclosure language is prepared.
- Athlete has time to review.
- Written agreement is prepared.
- Compliance questions are addressed.
