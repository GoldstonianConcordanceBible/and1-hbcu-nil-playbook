# Athlete Intake Form

## Purpose

This form helps HBCU athletes begin the NIL readiness process.

The intake form collects basic information about the athlete, their sport, personal brand, social media presence, NIL goals, financial literacy needs, sponsor interests, and compliance awareness.

This form can be used by:

- Athletes
- Mentors
- Alumni advisors
- Athletic departments
- NIL educators
- AND1 DAO contributors
- Financial literacy workshop leaders
- Sponsor development teams

## Core Principle

Before an athlete chases opportunities, the athlete should understand their identity, goals, needs, and responsibilities.

NIL readiness begins with self-awareness.
